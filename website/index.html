02 / Architecture & Security

Built like system software, not a prototype.

Every stage is engineered in pure native Swift against macOS system frameworks — Vision, Core ML, Core Image, LocalAuthentication, CryptoKit, and Keychain Services.

01
System Touch ID & Password Prompts
Hands-free Face ID approval across macOS System Settings, app installations, Saved Passwords, Finder admin dialogs, and Keychain — automatically excluding Apple Pay, purchases, and web page forms.
02
Backlit HDR Shadow Recovery
Real-time GPU Core Image tone mapping lifts crushed facial shadows when a bright sunlit window sits behind you, paired with backlight-aware Studio Light rim illumination that smoothly restores your display brightness.
03
Attention, Gaze & Chromatic Flash
Requires open eyes (EAR) and active screen gaze before unlocking, with optional randomized spectral reflection verification to defeat 4K video replay attacks.
04
Silent Adaptive Learning
Updates up to three rolling appearance slots from high-confidence 3D-verified unlocks, adapting effortlessly to glasses, facial hair, and seasonal lighting drift without re-enrolling.
05
Hardware-Backed AES-256-GCM
Touch ID gates a 256-bit session key stored strictly in your device-local macOS Keychain. Face embeddings, encrypted credentials, and intruder audit snapshots stay offline, and plaintext buffers are zeroed immediately after unlock.
06
Walk-Away Lock & Smart Clamshell
Optional zero-drain idle presence detection locks your Mac when you step away, and automatically switches cameras to external displays or Continuity Camera when your MacBook lid closes.
