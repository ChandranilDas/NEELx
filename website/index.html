Defense in Depth

Built like a system utility, not a prototype.

Every layer runs natively in Swift, Core ML, and Vision with strict memory hygiene and zero network dependencies.

01 · ILLUMINATION

Cinema-Smooth Dark-Room Lighting

Closed-loop 60fps cubic-eased backlight breathing paired with a feathered TrueTone ambient halo illuminates your face in pitch darkness, then dissolves back to your exact original screen brightness.

02 · ANTI-VIDEO REPLAY

Active Chromatic Flash

Optional randomized Cyan/Magenta reflection challenge verifies live photometric skin response in dim rooms — defeating even 4K OLED video replay attacks.

03 · ATTENTION

Require Attention & Eye Gaze

Measures Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and 3D head pose so your Mac stays locked while your eyes are closed or you're looking away from the screen.

04 · VAULT

Encrypted Security Audit Log

Records unlock events and compact JPEG snapshots of blocked intruders or spoofs encrypted with AES-256-GCM under your Touch ID-gated session key.

05 · ADAPTATION

Silent Adaptive Learning

Gradually adapts to glasses, beard growth, and seasonal lighting from high-confidence, 3D-verified unlocks — capped safely to protect your core 360° anchor.

06 · DESK PRESENCE

Walk-Away Auto-Lock & Clamshell

Optional zero-drain Presence Lock checks once after idle and locks your Mac if you stepped away, plus smart automatic switching to external or Continuity cameras when docked.
